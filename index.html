About Sunrise Village

A large, established 45+ community

Sunrise Village is a 207-home, age 45-and-over community at 1255 and 1260 Raymer Avenue, in Kelowna's Springfield/Spall area. The community is a mix of manufactured and built-on-site rancher-style homes — mostly 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom layouts, with individual floor plans (buyers and agents both refer to them by plan name, like the "D Plan") ranging from roughly 1,100 to over 1,500 square feet. It's centrally located, close to shopping, dining, medical services, and the Mission Creek Greenway — several homes back directly onto the creek.

Ownership Structure

What "land lease" actually means here

This is the single most important thing to understand before buying at Sunrise Village — and it's not complicated once it's explained clearly.

🔑
You own the home, not the land

When you buy at Sunrise Village, you own your home outright — but you lease the land it sits on, under a lease governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. This is a well-established, legally regulated structure, not an informal arrangement.

💰
A separate monthly lease fee

On top of any strata fee, expect a monthly land lease payment — we've seen figures in the roughly $600/month range on recent listings, though the exact amount depends on the specific lease and unit. We'll get you the real number for any home you're considering.

📅
Leases run for defined terms

Land leases here run on multi-year terms, not month-to-month. Knowing where a specific home's lease sits in its term is a genuinely important part of evaluating the purchase — we pull this before you make an offer, not after.

✅
Why buyers choose it anyway

Land-lease homes typically cost meaningfully less to buy than fee-simple equivalents nearby — a real, legitimate reason this community draws buyers who want 45+ community living without the price tag of owning the land outright.

This is general information, not legal advice — we strongly recommend having a real estate lawyer review the specific lease terms for any unit before you commit. We're happy to walk you through what we know first.
