Before You Buy

Rules & regulations at Sunrise Village

Every strata-organized, land-lease 45+ community runs on a set of bylaws that govern day-to-day life — age requirements, pets, rentals, exterior changes, and parking. This page gives you a realistic sense of the categories you'll need to review, in plain language. It is not a substitute for the actual bylaw documents, which we pull for every buyer before they write an offer.

What's Typically Covered

The main categories

🎂
Age restriction

Sunrise Village is confirmed age-restricted to 45 and over for the primary resident(s) on title. This is the one rule we can tell you with full confidence — it's consistent across the community and is verified as part of every purchase.

🐾
Pets

Like most communities of this type, pets are generally permitted with some form of restriction — typically around number and size. The exact wording of Sunrise Village's pet bylaw is something we confirm for you before you make an offer, especially if you have a specific pet situation in mind.

🔑
Rentals

Some land-lease and strata communities restrict long-term rentals or require board approval for tenants. If renting out your unit is part of your plan — now or down the road — this is one of the first things we check for the specific home you're considering.

🛠️
Exterior changes & renovations

Structural or exterior changes typically require strata approval before work begins. We've seen this firsthand at Sunrise Village — some C Plan owners have enclosed their carport into a single-car garage, which shows these changes are possible, but they go through an approval process rather than being done unilaterally.

🚙
Parking & RV storage

Most homes come with garage and/or driveway parking, and the community offers additional parking and RV storage. Availability and any rules around guest parking or storage assignment are worth confirming for your specific unit.

📜
Land lease terms

Separate from strata bylaws, each home's land lease has its own terms and remaining length. See our full land lease explainer for how that works and why it matters for resale.

This overview reflects general patterns common to strata-organized, land-lease 45+ communities and specific details we know about Sunrise Village — it is not a copy of the official bylaws, which vary and are amended over time. We pull the current, complete bylaw package and land lease document for any specific unit before you write an offer, and recommend having a real estate lawyer review both.
