Before You Buy

Rules & regulations at Sunrise Village

Sunrise Village is a private leasehold community — not a strata, and not governed by an HOA. It's 100% privately owned and operated, and the Rules & Regulations below are set and enforced directly by management. This page reflects what's actually in that document, in plain language. It is not a substitute for the official Rules & Regulations and land lease, which we pull for every buyer before they write an offer.

What's Typically Covered

The main categories

🎂
Age restriction

Sunrise Village enforces a strict minimum age of 45, with no exceptions — this applies to any additional occupant too, including a spouse, partner, or family member added later. Every resident meets with management to review and sign the Rules & Regulations before moving in.

✅
Buying process & park approval

Every offer at Sunrise Village is written subject to the buyer securing park approval before the subject is removed — that means an appointment with management to review and sign the Rules & Regulations. There's no Form B or strata documentation involved, since you're purchasing the home only, and the rules are strictly enforced with no exceptions.

🐾
Pets

One house pet is permitted, subject to management's approval. Cats must stay indoors or on a leash outside, dogs must be leashed at all times off your own property, and aggressive or nuisance behaviour can result in a pet losing its approval to stay. Guide and service dogs are permitted under BC's Guide Dog and Service Dog Act.

🔑
Rentals & subletting

Subletting a home requires specific approval from management before it happens — this isn't optional. If renting out your unit is part of your plan, now or down the road, this is one of the first things we check for the specific home you're considering.

🛠️
Exterior changes & renovations

Any exterior addition or alteration — carports, sheds, gazebos, solar panels, even new landscaping — requires a Renovation Request form submitted and approved by management before work begins. We've seen this firsthand at Sunrise Village: some C Plan owners have enclosed their carport into a garage, which shows these changes are possible, but they go through an approval process rather than being done unilaterally.

🚙
Parking & RV storage

Street parking generally isn't permitted — most homes come with driveway and/or garage parking, and the community has a separate RV storage compound, for an additional monthly fee, for larger vehicles, boats, and trucks. Guest and RV parking both have specific time limits and approval requirements, which we'll walk you through for your specific situation.

🏠
Home construction by phase

Sunrise Village includes a mix of manufactured, modular, and stick-built homes across its phases — Phase 1 homes are manufactured (identifiable by a CSA sticker), while the other phases mix modular and stick-built construction. The City of Kelowna has inspected homes in all phases except Phase 1. We confirm construction type for any specific home you're considering, since it can affect financing and insurance.

📜
Land lease terms

Separate from the community's Rules & Regulations, each home's land lease has its own terms and remaining length. See our full land lease explainer for how that works and why it matters for resale.

This overview reflects Sunrise Village's actual Rules & Regulations and specific details we know about the community — it is not a copy of the official document, which is amended periodically. We pull the current, complete Rules & Regulations and land lease document for any specific unit before you write an offer, and recommend having a real estate lawyer review both.
