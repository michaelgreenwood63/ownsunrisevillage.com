Is 45+ Land-Lease Living Right for You? The Honest Pros and Cons

November 2026 · By Mark Jontz & Associates · 5 min read

We specialize in Sunrise Village because we genuinely believe in what it offers — but no housing option is right for everyone, and buyers make better decisions with an honest list of trade-offs in front of them. Here it is.

The Real Advantages

  • Lower purchase price than an equivalent freehold or strata home, since you're not buying the land.
  • An established, active community — the clubhouse, pool, and hot tub create genuine day-to-day social life, not just a place to park a home.
  • Age restriction (45+) that many buyers specifically want, for a quieter, more settled neighbourhood feel.
  • Central location in Springfield/Spall, close to shopping, medical services, and the Mission Creek Greenway without the price tag of more central freehold neighbourhoods.
  • Simpler governance than a strata — no council votes, no AGMs, no special assessments tied to a shared building envelope.

The Real Trade-Offs

  • You don't own the land, and the ongoing lease fee doesn't build equity — it's a cost of occupying the site, same as rent, for as long as you live there.
  • Financing can be more involved, since not every lender treats land-lease properties the same way a freehold purchase is treated.
  • Selling involves an extra step — your buyer needs park approval before the sale can close, which can affect how quickly a deal comes together.
  • The lease fee can increase periodically, governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, which bounds the increase but doesn't eliminate it.
  • Age restriction cuts both ways — it's exactly what some buyers want, and a dealbreaker for others who might want to have younger family stay long-term.

Our honest recommendation: if the advantages above genuinely match what you're looking for in this stage of life, Sunrise Village is one of the stronger established options in Kelowna for it. If the land-lease structure or the age restriction gives you pause, it's worth seriously comparing against freehold or strata alternatives before committing — we'll tell you that directly rather than talk you past a real concern.
