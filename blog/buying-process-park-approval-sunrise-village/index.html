Park Approval at Sunrise Village: How Buying Actually Works

September 2026 · By Mark Jontz & Associates · 5 min read

Buying at Sunrise Village has one extra step most Kelowna real estate purchases don't: park approval. It's not complicated, but it does need to happen in the right order, and skipping it — or not understanding it — is the fastest way to slow down a deal. Here's exactly how it works.

The Short Version

Every offer at Sunrise Village is written subject to the buyer securing park approval from management before the subject is removed. In practice, that means the buyer sits down with Sunrise Village's on-site property manager, reviews the community's Rules & Regulations, and signs off on them — before the deal is finalized, not after possession.

Step by Step

  • 1. We write the offer with a park approval subject. This protects the buyer — if approval doesn't come through for some reason, the deal doesn't proceed.
  • 2. The buyer books an appointment with management. This is a short, straightforward meeting, not an interview or a credit check — it's about making sure every incoming resident has actually read and understood the community's rules before they own a home here.
  • 3. The Rules & Regulations get reviewed and signed. The buyer receives a copy showing park approval is secured, which satisfies the subject condition.
  • 4. Subject removal and the rest of the transaction proceeds normally from there — financing, inspection, and any other standard subjects follow the usual timeline alongside this one.
  • 5. After possession, Sunrise Village's administration sends the necessary transfer paperwork to the buyer's lawyer or notary, typically within about two weeks of the possession date.

Why this matters for your offer timeline: park approval isn't something you can do the day before closing. We build enough time into every offer we write at Sunrise Village to get this appointment booked and completed comfortably before subject removal — it's one of the first things we schedule once an offer is accepted.

What Management Is Actually Checking

There's no strata corporation and no HOA at Sunrise Village — it's 100% privately owned leasehold land, and the Rules & Regulations are set and enforced directly by the operator. Park approval exists to confirm two things: that every resident meets the community's minimum age requirement of 45, with no exceptions, and that incoming owners understand the rules they're agreeing to live under — pets, exterior changes, parking, and everything else covered in our Rules & Regulations overview. There's no Form B and no strata documentation involved, since you're purchasing the home only.

Why This Is a Good Thing, Not a Red Flag

First-time land-lease buyers sometimes hear "subject to park approval" and assume it's a hurdle unique to Sunrise Village that signals risk. In practice, it's the opposite — it's part of what keeps the community consistent and the age restriction genuinely enforceable, which is exactly what draws a lot of buyers here in the first place. We've walked more than 50 transactions through this exact process, and it's never been the part that held up a deal when it was planned for properly.
