About the Team

Mark Jontz & Associates built OwnSunriseVillage.com as a dedicated resource for this community specifically — including the land-lease details that generic Kelowna real estate sites tend to gloss over.

We currently have two active listings inside Sunrise Village, and we track this community's lease terms, floor plans, and amenities closely. Whether you're buying your first 45+ home or selling one you've loved for years, we'll give you straight answers.
