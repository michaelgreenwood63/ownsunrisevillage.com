The Clubhouse

The heart of the community

Ask anyone who's owned at Sunrise Village for more than a season what they'd tell a buyer, and the clubhouse comes up almost every time. It's not a room that sits empty — it's the reason a lot of residents describe this as a real community rather than just a place they park a home. Here's what's actually in it.

Warmer Months

Outdoor pool & hot tub

🏊
The heated outdoor pool

A consistent draw mentioned across current listings — it's the single most-cited amenity when residents talk about why they chose Sunrise Village over other 45+ options in the area. Open through the warmer months, it's a genuine social spot, not just a place to swim laps.

♨️
The hot tub

Right alongside the pool — a year-round draw for residents who want the soak without the swim, especially through Kelowna's colder months.

Indoors

Fitness, library & billiards

💪
Fitness Room

On-site fitness facilities inside the community centre — no need to drive to a gym for a basic workout, part of what makes daily routines easier for residents who want to stay active close to home.

🎱
Billiards Room

A dedicated indoor social space with a pool table — one of the regular spots residents gather, especially through the winter when the pool and hot tub see less traffic.

📚
Library

A quiet common-area space for residents — part of the same community centre as the fitness room and billiards room.

🍽️
Common Kitchen & Gathering Space

Common space with a kitchen for resident gatherings — the kind of shared space that turns a park of individual homes into an actual neighbourhood.

Why Residents Stay

Why people love living at Sunrise Village

We track this community closely, and a few things come up again and again — from current owners, from listing remarks, and from buyers who come back to us after visiting.

🤝
It's a real neighbourhood

With 207 homes and a clubhouse that actually gets used, residents get to know each other in a way that's harder to find in a standard subdivision. It shows up consistently in how current owners describe the community.

🧘
Low-maintenance living, without giving up space

Rancher-style and manufactured homes mean no stairs and a smaller footprint to maintain — while still getting a real yard, garage, and often 1,000+ square feet, which is more than most condo or townhome alternatives at a similar price point.

🌳
Creekside privacy on select homes

Several homes back directly onto Mission Creek — genuinely rare in a community at this scale and price point, and a specific detail buyers ask us about by name.

📍
Everything's actually close

Shopping, dining, and medical services are all nearby in the Springfield/Spall area — residents aren't trading community for convenience. See our location breakdown for specifics.

One Practical Note

Clubhouse access & specific policies

Exact clubhouse hours, booking rules for private events, and any guest policies are set by the strata and can vary slightly over time. We'll get you the current, exact rules for whichever home you're looking at — this page is here to give you a real sense of what's available, not to replace the official strata documentation.
