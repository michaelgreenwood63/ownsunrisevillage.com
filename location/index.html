Where It Sits

1255 & 1260 Raymer Avenue, Kelowna

Sunrise Village sits in Kelowna's Springfield/Spall area — a central, established part of the city rather than an outlying suburb. That location is a big part of the draw for 45+ buyers who want to downsize without moving away from everyday conveniences.

Getting Around

What's actually nearby

Springfield/Spall is one of Kelowna's more central neighbourhoods — here's what's realistically close by car.

🛒
Everyday shopping

Orchard Park Shopping Centre and the surrounding Springfield Road retail strip are a short drive away — groceries, pharmacy, and big-box shopping without a long commute.

🏥
Medical services

Kelowna General Hospital and a range of medical clinics are within easy reach — a meaningful factor for many 45+ buyers evaluating where to settle.

🌲
Mission Creek Greenway

The Greenway trail system is close by, and several homes in the community back directly onto Mission Creek itself — walkable nature access most communities can't offer.

🏙️
Downtown Kelowna & the lake

Downtown, Okanagan Lake, and Pandosy Village are all a manageable drive away — close enough for regular trips without living in the middle of downtown traffic and density.

🍽️
Dining

A wide range of restaurants along Springfield Road and the surrounding area, from quick everyday spots to sit-down dining.

✈️
Kelowna International Airport

A straightforward drive north via Springfield/Highway 97 — convenient for residents who travel or have family flying in to visit.

Exact drive times vary with traffic and the specific unit's location within the community. We're happy to walk the route with you before you buy.
